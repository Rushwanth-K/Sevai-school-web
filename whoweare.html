Our Story

Over two decades of shaping confident, capable learners

Established in 1997–98, SEVAI Shanthi Matriculation Higher Secondary School has grown from a small institution in Pettavaithalai into one of the most trusted schools in the Tiruchirappalli region. Today, we serve over 1,800 students from Pre-KG through Class 12, guided by more than 72 qualified and dedicated teachers.

Affiliated to the State Board and recognized by the Director of Matriculation Schools, Chennai, our school follows an English-medium curriculum that blends child-centred learning with academic rigour.

We are proud of our 100% board results over three consecutive years — a reflection of the trust our students and families place in us every day.
